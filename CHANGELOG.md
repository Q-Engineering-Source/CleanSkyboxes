# NeoforgeSkyboxes 0.1.1

## Fixed

- Fixed brief client-side lag spikes when entering a biome or other region that activates a skybox for the first time.
- Skybox textures are now loaded during resource loading instead of during their first rendered frame.
- Texture preloading covers Nuit/FabricSkyBoxes skyboxes, animated and multi-texture skyboxes, custom sun and moon textures, and OptiFine/MCPatcher sky layers.
